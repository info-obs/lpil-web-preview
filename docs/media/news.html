Expansion 20 June 2026

Laborate Opens New R&D Centre in Chandigarh, Strengthening India's Pharmaceutical Innovation Pipeline

"Our new Research and Development Centre represents a deliberate investment in India's pharmaceutical innovation ecosystem — designed to translate scientific insight into medicines that reach patients faster, at higher quality, and at accessible cost." — Director of Research & Development, Laborate Pharmaceuticals India Ltd.

Laborate Pharmaceuticals India Ltd. has inaugurated its new Research & Development Centre in Chandigarh, Haryana, marking a major step in the company's transition from a contract manufacturer to an innovation-driven pharmaceutical enterprise. The centre houses formulation development laboratories, stability study suites, analytical chemistry divisions, and regulatory affairs coordination units — designed to support both generic development and biosimilar research programmes for the Indian and export markets.
