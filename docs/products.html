Our Product Portfolio

Built to Address the World’s Healthcare Needs

Laborate Pharmaceuticals manufactures a comprehensive range of pharmaceutical formulations covering the most prevalent acute and chronic disease categories. Our portfolio spans four business divisions — Elite, Aqualab, GPP and CDMO — each with a distinct therapeutic focus and market channel.

Every product in our portfolio is manufactured under WHO-GMP conditions, tested against British Pharmacopoeia (BP), Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP) or United States Pharmacopoeia (USP) standards, and supported by a dedicated quality team from API receipt to final batch release.

Quality & Regulatory

Every Batch. Every Market. Every Standard.

Our quality management system governs every stage of manufacturing, from raw material qualification to post-market pharmacovigilance.

WHO-GMP Certification

All Laborate manufacturing sites are certified under World Health Organization Good Manufacturing Practice guidelines — the global benchmark for pharmaceutical manufacturing quality.

BP / IP / USP Standards

Product specifications are validated against British Pharmacopoeia (BP), Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP) and United States Pharmacopoeia (USP) as required by the target market and product category.

35+ Market Registrations

Product dossiers prepared and registered in compliance with local regulatory authorities — CDSCO (India), NAFDAC (Nigeria), DSCG (multiple), EU and others across Africa, CIS and MENA.

ICH-Compliant Stability Testing

All products subjected to real-time and accelerated stability studies under ICH Q1A/Q1B guidelines. Shelf-life data submitted as part of dossier packages for all export markets.
