Our Story

Four Decades of Scientific Excellence

Founded in 1985 in Panipat, Haryana, Laborate Pharmaceuticals began as a small manufacturing unit with a singular ambition: to produce medicines of international quality at prices accessible to all. Forty years later, we operate five GMP-certified plants, employ more than 5,000 professionals, and distribute over 1,500 therapeutic products across 35 countries on every inhabited continent.

Our growth has never been accidental. Every decade has been defined by deliberate investment — in technology, in talent, and in the trust of the healthcare professionals and patients who depend on us. From our earliest WHO-GMP certification in 1995 to our state-of-the-art R&D centre inaugurated in 2010, each milestone reflects a commitment to doing things right, even when faster or cheaper alternatives were available.

Today, Laborate stands as one of India's most trusted mid-sized pharmaceutical manufacturers — lean enough to innovate rapidly, large enough to deliver at scale, and focused enough to maintain the standards our patients deserve.

  • WHO-GMP Certified since 1995
  • ISO 9001 & ISO 14001 Accredited
  • 35+ International Markets
  • 200+ Active R&D Projects

What We Stand For

Core Values

Six principles that shape every decision we make — from the laboratory bench to the boardroom.

Innovation

We invest in tomorrow's medicines today. Our dedicated R&D infrastructure continuously explores novel formulations, delivery mechanisms, and therapeutic combinations that advance patient outcomes.

Quality

Quality is not a department — it is our culture. Every batch across our five manufacturing plants is held to standards that exceed global regulatory benchmarks, from formulation to final release.

Integrity

We operate with complete transparency toward regulators, partners, and the communities we serve. Our record of zero regulatory enforcement actions reflects the integrity woven into our processes.

People First

Our 5,000+ professionals are our greatest competitive advantage. We invest in their development, well-being, and growth because excellent medicines begin with excellent people.

Accessibility

A medicine that cannot be afforded cannot heal. We engineer our supply chains and pricing to ensure our products remain within reach of patients in tier-2 cities, rural areas, and low-income markets.

Sustainability

We are committed to responsible manufacturing — reducing our environmental footprint, minimising waste, and building a business that future generations of healthcare professionals will be proud to inherit.

Innovation & Research

Where Science Meets Possibility

At Laborate, research is not a cost centre — it is our future. Our R&D Centre in Panipat houses more than 200 active projects across formulation science, bioequivalence studies, and exploratory pharmaceutical research. Our scientists work at the intersection of chemistry, biology, and engineering — developing next-generation drug delivery systems that improve compliance, efficacy, and tolerability.

We collaborate with leading academic institutions across India and partner with global contract research organisations to accelerate the development timeline from molecule to medicine. Our analytical laboratory is equipped with HPLC, GC-MS, and dissolution testing systems that meet ICH Q2(R1) analytical validation standards.

Innovation at Laborate extends beyond the laboratory. Our manufacturing engineering teams apply the same scientific discipline to process optimisation — continuously improving yield, efficiency, and sustainability across our production network.
