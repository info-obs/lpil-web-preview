Export Expertise

End-to-End International Capability.

International Logistics

Air Freight Sea Freight DDP/DAP

Multi-modal freight solutions — air and sea — with 98.2% on-time dispatch rate. DDP, DAP, and FOB terms available for all major Incoterms.

Export Documentation

COA FSC GMP Certificates

Complete export documentation — COA, Certificate of Free Sale, GMP certificates, regulatory dossiers, and country-specific import permits.

Regulatory Compliance

WHO-GMP PIC/S USFDA Ready

WHO-GMP certified facilities, PIC/S-aligned quality systems, and pre-approval inspection readiness for USFDA, EU, and regulated market submissions.

Serialisation & Track-Trace

GS1 Barcoding Aggregation DSCSA Ready

GS1-compliant serialisation, 2D barcode printing, and aggregation for EU Falsified Medicines Directive, DSCSA, and country-specific track-trace requirements.

Cold Chain Management

2–8°C Chain GDP Compliant Reefer Freight

Temperature-controlled logistics for cold-chain products — 2–8°C integrity maintained from manufacturing to destination, with GDP-compliant documentation.

Export Packaging

Multi-Language Artwork Mgmt Country-Specific

Market-specific label printing, multi-language patient leaflets, and complete artwork management — compliant with destination country labelling regulations.
