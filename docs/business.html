Common Questions

Answers for Procurement & BD Teams.

Practical answers to the most common questions from potential partners, distributors, and procurement offices.

What are the minimum order quantities for contract manufacturing?

MOQs vary by product type, dosage form, and manufacturing complexity. For oral solid dosage forms, typical MOQs begin at 500,000 tablets or capsules per batch. Sterile injectable formulations carry higher thresholds due to production line requirements. Our business development team can confirm specific MOQs based on your product specification.

What regulatory documentation does Laborate provide for export markets?

Standard documentation includes Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (CPP), Certificate of GMP compliance, product registration dossiers (CTD/eCTD format), stability data packages, analytical method validations, and batch records. Our regulatory team supports dossier submission across 60+ markets.

Which international markets does Laborate currently supply?

Laborate maintains active export operations across 35+ countries in South Asia, Middle East and North Africa, Sub-Saharan Africa, Central Asia, Southeast Asia, and Latin America. A full market list with registration status is available on request from our Business Development team.

Can Laborate assist with product registration in new markets?

Yes. Our regulatory affairs team provides end-to-end support for new market registration — CTD dossier preparation, submission management, and liaison with local regulatory authorities across 60+ markets. We can advise on the most efficient registration pathway for your target market.

What does a partner quality audit of Laborate's facilities involve?

Laborate welcomes partner quality audits. Our standard programme includes facility walkthrough, QMS documentation review, access to batch records and deviation reports, regulatory inspection history, and direct dialogue with Quality Assurance leadership. We maintain open-book transparency with all manufacturing partners.

What CDMO services does Laborate offer for novel formulations?

Our CDMO capability covers the full development lifecycle: formulation design, pre-formulation studies, analytical method development and validation, pilot and scale-up manufacturing, ICH stability studies, bioequivalence study coordination, and commercial-scale production. Core expertise in oral solid dosage, liquids, and sterile injectables.
