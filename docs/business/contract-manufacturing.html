Our Capabilities

End-to-End Manufacturing Services.

From initial formulation work through technology transfer, regulatory filing, and ongoing commercial supply — a single manufacturing partner for the full product lifecycle.

Formulation Development

API compatibility studies, excipient selection, prototype batch development, and accelerated stability testing to establish validated manufacturing specifications for your product.

Technology Transfer

Structured transfer from your existing manufacturer or R&D lab to our facilities — including batch record review, method bridging, equivalence confirmation, and full documentation handover.

Analytical Development

Method development and validation (HPLC, UV, dissolution, microbiological) in full compliance with ICH Q2 guidelines and the pharmacopoeial requirements of your target markets.

Regulatory Documentation

DMF preparation, CTD dossier compilation, COPP applications, WHO prequalification submissions, and registration file management for 35+ global markets.

Manufacturing

Full-scale commercial manufacturing under WHO-GMP conditions. Dedicated manufacturing slots, validated equipment, and batch traceability from raw material receipt through finished goods release.

Packaging

Primary and secondary packaging across blister, strip, bottle, ampoule, vial, and sachet configurations with full packaging validation, line clearance records, and reconciliation.

Serialization

End-to-end serialization and track-and-trace compliance for EU FMD, US DSCSA, and other regulated markets — with integration options for your existing serialization platform.

Artwork Support

Label and carton artwork design, regulatory review, approval management, and version control aligned to your target market's regulatory labelling requirements and brand standards.

Commercial Supply

Ongoing commercial supply management including rolling forecast planning, safety stock maintenance, priority batch scheduling, and on-time dispatch performance reporting.

From Enquiry to Commercial Supply.

Day 1

1

Enquiry

Submit your manufacturing enquiry — product details, target dosage form, regulatory markets, and indicative volume requirements.

Days 2–5

2

Week 2

3

Weeks 2–3

4

Quality & Compliance

Manufacturing to the Highest Regulatory Standards.

Every batch manufactured at Laborate is backed by a comprehensive quality system aligned to international regulatory expectations — from WHO-GMP through USFDA and PIC/S.

WHO-GMP Certification

All five manufacturing facilities hold valid WHO-GMP certificates. Inspections completed on schedule — active certificates maintained for all registered export markets.

ISO 9001:2015

Quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015, covering procurement, manufacturing, in-process testing, storage, and dispatch across all product categories.

USFDA Readiness

API manufacturing sites hold USFDA Drug Master File (DMF) registrations. Finished dosage facilities maintained to USFDA inspection standards for IND and ANDA submissions.

PIC/S Guidelines

Manufacturing practices align with PIC/S Guide to GMP, supporting market registrations across the European Union, Africa, South-East Asia, and Asia-Pacific.

Documentation Systems

Complete batch manufacturing records, validation master plans, stability protocols, deviations register, and change control managed in a validated document management system.

Regulatory Audit Support

Dedicated regulatory affairs team supports partner-initiated audits, national authority pre-approval inspections, and annual product reviews with full documentation access.
