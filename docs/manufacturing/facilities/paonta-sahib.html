Plant Overview

Purpose-Built for Sterile & Export Manufacturing

Established in 1998, the Paonta Sahib Sterile & Export Plant is Laborate's flagship facility for sterile pharmaceutical manufacturing. Located in the pharmaceutical hub of Paonta Sahib, Himachal Pradesh, this plant specialises in injectables, ophthalmic solutions and export-grade formulations destined for regulated markets globally.

The facility operates under Class A/B cleanroom environments for all sterile manufacturing operations, with full environmental monitoring, validated aseptic processing lines and an on-site NABL-accredited QC laboratory. All production is executed under WHO GMP, USFDA and EU GMP frameworks simultaneously.

With 6 active manufacturing lines and a daily output capacity approaching 2 million units, Paonta Sahib supplies products to over 25 countries across Africa, Southeast Asia, Latin America and the European Union.

Quality Assurance

On-Site Quality Laboratories

The Paonta Sahib facility operates a fully integrated, on-site quality control laboratory that handles all testing requirements from incoming raw material inspection through in-process controls to final product release. The laboratory is NABL-accredited under ISO/IEC 17025 and operates independently from the manufacturing floor.

All analytical methods are validated to ICH Q2(R1) guidelines. Microbiological testing is performed in a dedicated, classified laboratory separate from the main QC block. Environmental monitoring of classified manufacturing areas is conducted on a continuous schedule.

The facility maintains a reference sample library for all batches manufactured, with retention periods exceeding regulatory requirements for all export markets supplied.

  • HPLC / UPLC — 8 units (Waters, Agilent)
  • ICP-MS for elemental impurities
  • GC / GC-MS for residual solvents
  • Automated dissolution testing apparatus
  • Particle size analyser (laser diffraction)
  • Microbiological cleanroom testing suites
