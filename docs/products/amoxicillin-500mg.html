Product Description

Pharmacological Action

Amoxicillin is a semi-synthetic, broad-spectrum penicillin antibiotic. It exerts its bactericidal action by inhibiting the biosynthesis of bacterial cell wall peptidoglycan, making it effective against a wide range of Gram-positive and selected Gram-negative organisms.

Indications

Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules are indicated for the treatment of:

  • Acute bacterial infections of the upper and lower respiratory tract (including community-acquired pneumonia and acute exacerbation of chronic bronchitis)
  • Uncomplicated urinary tract infections caused by susceptible organisms
  • Skin and soft tissue infections
  • Dental and oral infections
  • Typhoid fever and other Salmonella infections (where susceptibility is confirmed)

Dosage & Administration

Adults and children over 40 kg: 250–500 mg every 8 hours or 750 mg–1 g every 12 hours (depending on severity). Treatment course typically 5–10 days. Always follow prescriber directions and local antimicrobial stewardship guidelines.

Manufacturing Standards

Manufactured at Laborate’s Panipat facility under WHO-GMP conditions using API sourced from audited, GMP-certified suppliers. Each batch undergoes rigorous in-process quality control and finished-product testing against British Pharmacopoeia (BP) and Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP) standards before release.

Contraindications & Precautions

Contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to penicillins or any component of the formulation. Cross-reactivity with cephalosporins may occur in sensitive patients. Use with caution in renal impairment; dosage adjustment may be required. Not recommended in infectious mononucleosis (risk of rash).
