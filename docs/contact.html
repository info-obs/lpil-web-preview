Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Can't find your answer here? Use the contact form above or call us directly.

How do I enquire about Laborate products for my market?

Use the contact form above and select "Product Enquiry" as the enquiry type. Include your country, the therapeutic area of interest, and any specific product names or categories. Our Sales & Marketing or Export Business team will respond within 2 business days.

How can I apply for a business partnership or distribution agreement?

Select "Business Partnership" from the enquiry type dropdown in the contact form. Include your company profile, target markets, and specific collaboration interests. Our Business Development team will evaluate your enquiry and revert with next steps.

What certifications and approvals does Laborate hold?

Laborate Pharmaceuticals holds WHO-GMP certification, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, and multiple national and international regulatory approvals. For detailed certification documentation, visit our Quality page or submit a regulatory enquiry through the contact form.

How do I report an adverse drug reaction?

Adverse drug reactions or product quality complaints should be reported immediately to our Pharmacovigilance team at pv@laborate.com. Alternatively, use the contact form and select "Pharmacovigilance" as the enquiry type. We treat all safety reports as priority cases.

How long does it take to receive a response to my enquiry?

Standard enquiries receive a response within 2 business days. Pharmacovigilance and safety reports are treated as priority and handled immediately. For urgent matters, please call our main line at +91-180-3508200 during office hours (Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 6 PM IST).

How do I register as a vendor or supplier?

Submit your enquiry via the contact form with "Vendor / Supplier" selected as the type. Include your company profile, product or service categories, and relevant certifications (ISO, GMP). Our Procurement team will review your application and initiate the vendor evaluation process.

Where can I find career opportunities at Laborate?

All current openings are listed on our Careers page. You can filter by department, location, and experience level. For general HR enquiries, email hr@laborate.com.

Does Laborate provide product samples for evaluation?

Product samples may be available for qualified business enquiries subject to regulatory compliance and minimum quantity thresholds. Submit a Product Enquiry through the contact form with your business details, country, and the specific product you wish to evaluate. Our Sales team will assess your request.

Need Immediate Assistance?

Call us directly at +91-180-3508200 or email info@laborate.com.
