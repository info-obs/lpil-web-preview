Manufacturing Base

North India — The Source of Every Shipment

All Laborate products exported worldwide originate from our WHO-GMP certified manufacturing campus spanning three strategic locations in northern India — Panipat and Karnal in Haryana, and Paonta Sahib on the Himachal Pradesh border.

Panipat, Haryana

Head office & primary export manufacturing campus. Solid dosage forms, liquids, and injectables. WHO-GMP certified, ISO 9001:2015.

Karnal, Haryana

Secondary manufacturing and packaging site. Speciality tablets, capsules, and nutraceuticals. WHO-GMP certified, dedicated OSD block.

Paonta Sahib, Himachal Pradesh

Excise-exempt Himachal facility. Ophthalmics, injectables, and regulated-market product lines. WHO-GMP, PIC/S-aligned QMS.
