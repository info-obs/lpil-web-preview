  • Information about a product's therapeutic area, mechanism of action, or clinical profile should not be interpreted as confirmation that the product is approved for those uses in your country.

Regulatory Authorities

In India, Laborate's products are regulated by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940. In each export market, products are subject to the regulations of the relevant national competent authority — such as the MHRA (UK), EMA (EU), TGA (Australia), FDA (USA), or equivalent. Approval in one jurisdiction does not imply approval in others.
