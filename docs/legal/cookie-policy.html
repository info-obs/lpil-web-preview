Introduction

This Cookie Policy ("Policy") explains how Laborate Pharmaceuticals India Ltd. ("Laborate", "we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar browser technologies when you visit our corporate website at laborate.com.

We believe in transparency. This Policy tells you clearly what cookies we currently use, what we do not use, why each cookie exists, and how you can control your preferences — both now and through the consent management system we will deploy as part of our WordPress implementation.

This Policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal information more broadly, and our Terms & Conditions, which govern your use of the website.

Our Current Cookie Footprint Is Minimal

Laborate currently uses only Google Analytics 4 for website analytics, plus strictly necessary cookies for basic website operation. We do not use advertising cookies, remarketing pixels, social media trackers, behavioural profiling, or personalisation technologies. A full cookie consent management system will be deployed in a future phase — see Section 10.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places in your web browser or on your device when you visit a page. They are universally used across the web to make websites work efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide website operators with information about how their sites are used.

Not all data stored by a website is a "cookie" in the traditional sense. Modern browsers support several storage mechanisms, each with different properties:

Session Cookie
A temporary cookie that exists only for the duration of your browser session. It is deleted automatically when you close your browser tab or window. Session cookies are typically used for strictly necessary purposes such as maintaining a form state or security token.
Persistent Cookie
A cookie that remains on your device for a specified period (defined by an expiry date set by the website) or until you manually delete it. Analytics cookies such as Google Analytics' _ga are persistent, typically lasting 1–2 years.
First-Party Cookie
A cookie set directly by the website domain you are visiting (laborate.com). First-party cookies are generally more privacy-preserving because they are scoped to the single site you are browsing.
Third-Party Cookie
A cookie set by a domain other than the website you are visiting — typically by an embedded analytics, advertising, or social media service. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies in the laborate.com domain, but the data is transmitted to Google's servers for processing.
localStorage / sessionStorage
Browser-native key-value storage APIs that function similarly to cookies but are stored locally in the browser and are not automatically transmitted to the server. Used by some analytics frameworks and modern web applications. Our future consent management system will use localStorage to store your consent preferences.

Why We Use Cookies

Laborate uses a deliberately minimal set of cookies to achieve specific, justified purposes. We do not use cookies as a tool for surveillance, tracking across other websites, building behavioural profiles, or serving targeted advertising.

Our current and future cookie use is limited to the following purposes:

  • Website operation — Strictly necessary session management and security tokens that enable the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled without breaking core website functionality.
  • Analytics — Understanding how visitors navigate our website in aggregate, which pages are most visited, and where we can improve content and usability. We currently use Google Analytics 4 for this purpose, with consent gating where required by applicable law.
  • Consent management (future) — Storing your cookie preferences between visits so that you are not re-prompted on every page. This will be implemented when the full consent management system is deployed.
  • Functional preferences (future) — Remembering optional preferences such as language or region to improve your experience. Currently not deployed.
  • Security and load balancing — Server-set session tokens used for infrastructure purposes (e.g., load balancer affinity) that are strictly necessary and carry no personal data.

We do not use cookies for advertising, remarketing, cross-site tracking, behavioural profiling, or any other purpose beyond those listed above. See Section 6 for an explicit list of what we do not do.

5.2 Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics 4)

Google Analytics 4 sets the following cookies to help us understand how visitors use laborate.com. These cookies are loaded only with your consent where required by applicable law. All data is collected in anonymised, aggregated form — individual visitor identities are not shared with Laborate.

How We Configure Google Analytics 4

Our GA4 implementation anonymises IP addresses before transmission to Google's servers. Data retention in the GA4 property is set to 14 months. We do not enable Google Signals, advertising features, or cross-device tracking in GA4. GA4 data is not shared with Google for advertising purposes. Cookie names and identifiers may change as Google updates the GA4 platform — this table reflects current known names.

Cookies We Do Not Use

We want to be equally transparent about what we do not do. The following technologies and cookie types are explicitly not in use on laborate.com:

  • Advertising cookies — We do not use cookies to serve targeted, contextual, or programmatic advertisements on this or any other website.
  • Behavioural profiling — We do not build profiles of individual users based on browsing history, content consumption, or behavioural patterns.
  • Remarketing pixels — We do not use Meta Pixel, Google Ads conversion tags, LinkedIn Insight Tag, or any similar remarketing technology to track visitors after they leave our website.
  • Affiliate tracking — We do not participate in affiliate marketing programmes and do not use cookies to track referrals or commissions.
  • Cross-site tracking — We do not use technologies designed to track individuals across multiple websites or applications.
  • Heatmapping and session recording — We do not use Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, FullStory, or any session recording or heatmapping service.
  • A/B testing platforms — No split-testing or multivariate testing tools are currently deployed.
  • CRM tracking pixels — No HubSpot, Salesforce, or similar CRM tracking scripts are currently embedded in the website.
  • Chat widget trackers — No live chat platform (Intercom, Zendesk, Drift, etc.) is currently deployed on the website.
  • Sale of data — We do not sell, rent, or share cookie data or browsing data with third parties for their commercial purposes.

Our Privacy Commitment

Laborate is a pharmaceutical manufacturer — not an advertising business. Our website exists to serve healthcare professionals, business partners, and the public with accurate corporate and product information. We will never use visitor data for advertising, profiling, or purposes beyond those described in this Policy and our Privacy Policy.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options for managing cookies on laborate.com. Your choices can be made at different levels — through our website's own consent tools (when deployed), or through your browser settings.

8.1 Cookie Preference Centre (Coming Soon)

When our full consent management system is deployed, you will be able to manage your cookie preferences at any time through our Cookie Preference Centre. This will allow you to:

  • View all cookie categories in use on laborate.com
  • Toggle individual categories on or off (except Strictly Necessary, which cannot be disabled)
  • Save and update your preferences at any time
  • Review the specific cookies and third-party services within each category

A "Cookie Preferences" link will be permanently available in the website footer on all pages.

8.2 Browser Settings

All major web browsers provide controls for managing cookies. You can use your browser to:

  • Block all cookies (note: this may break the functionality of many websites, including some features of laborate.com)
  • Block third-party cookies only (less disruptive, still reduces some tracking)
  • Delete all existing cookies stored on your device
  • Delete cookies from a specific website
  • Set up notifications when a website attempts to set a cookie

Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Browser cookie settings can be found in: Chrome — Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies. Firefox — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data. Safari — Preferences → Privacy. Edge — Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions. Opera — Settings → Advanced → Privacy & Security → Cookies. These menus allow you to block, allow, or delete cookies per site or globally. Settings may vary by browser version.

8.3 Opt-Out of Google Analytics

If you wish to opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites you visit, Google provides a browser add-on for this purpose. The Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on prevents the Google Analytics JavaScript from sharing information about your visit with Google Analytics. This is separate from our own consent mechanism and applies globally across all GA-enabled websites.

Cookie Consent

Where required by applicable law — including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the EU ePrivacy Directive — we obtain your consent before activating non-essential cookies.

10.1 Current State

Our full cookie consent management system is not yet deployed. Our current implementation loads Google Analytics 4 in accordance with our legal assessment of applicable requirements. Where GA4 requires consent (for example, for visitors from EU member states), we will implement appropriate consent gating as part of our full CMP deployment.

10.2 Future Consent System

When our Cookie Preference Centre is deployed, the consent experience on laborate.com will work as follows:

  1. First visit — A cookie consent banner will be displayed, asking you to make a choice. No non-essential cookies will be activated until you have made a choice or the banner is displayed.
  2. Your choices — The banner will offer three options: Accept All (activates all enabled cookie categories), Reject Non-Essential (only strictly necessary cookies remain active), and Manage Preferences (opens the Cookie Preference Centre for granular per-category control).
  3. Saving preferences — Your consent choice is stored in your browser's localStorage under a Laborate-specific key. This record includes the consent version (tied to this Cookie Policy's version number) and the timestamp of your choice.
  4. Consent validity — Consent records will be valid for 12 months. After this period, you will be asked to confirm your preferences again.
  5. Policy updates — If we make material changes to this Cookie Policy or add new cookie categories, the consent version number will change and you will be prompted to review and re-confirm your preferences.
  6. No retroactive consent — If you select "Reject Non-Essential", non-essential cookies will not be activated, regardless of any previously accepted version.

Cookie Consent System: Not Yet Active

The cookie consent banner and Preference Centre described in this section are planned for deployment in a future phase of the laborate.com development programme. Until that system is live, you can manage your cookie preferences using your browser settings (see Section 8.2) or by opting out of Google Analytics using Google's opt-out tool (see Section 8.3).

Withdrawing Consent

You may withdraw or modify your cookie consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of any processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

Current Options

  • Browser cookie settings — Use your browser's cookie management controls to block or delete cookies from laborate.com at any time (see Section 8.2). This immediately prevents further cookie-based tracking but does not retroactively delete data already collected.
  • Google Analytics opt-out — Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent your browser from sending data to Google Analytics across all websites. This is effective immediately upon installation.
  • Incognito / Private browsing mode — Using your browser's private browsing mode means cookies are not stored after you close the browser window. Note that this does not prevent cookies from being set during the session itself.
  • Contact us — If you have questions about your data or wish to request deletion of analytics data attributable to your device, please contact us at legal@laborate.com. Note that because GA4 stores only anonymous identifiers, we may not be able to identify and delete data linked to a specific individual without additional context.

Future Options (When Consent System Is Deployed)

  • Cookie Preference Centre — Accessible via the "Cookie Preferences" link in the website footer. Allows you to toggle individual categories and save updated preferences instantly.
  • Re-prompt on version change — If we update this Cookie Policy in a material way, you will be automatically re-prompted to review and update your preferences.
  • Revoke all — Selecting "Reject Non-Essential" in the Cookie Preference Centre immediately stops all non-essential scripts and cookies from operating in your browser session.
