Our Story

The Elite Story

Founded in 1992 as Laborate Pharmaceuticals' first branded prescription division, Elite was built to serve the clinical needs of specialist physicians across India's secondary care hospitals. At a time when quality antibiotic formulations were largely imported or inconsistently available, Elite changed the landscape — bringing hospital-grade medicines to tier-2 and tier-3 markets.

Three decades later, Elite has grown into a 400+ product portfolio spanning antibiotics, cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, pain management, and vitamins. Every product in the Elite range is manufactured at WHO GMP-certified facilities with ISO 9001:2015 quality systems and exported to 25+ countries.

Elite remains the cornerstone of Laborate's prescription business — a brand that specialist physicians trust not just for efficacy, but for the consistency of every tablet, every capsule, every batch.

"Every Elite product is a promise — to the physician who prescribes it, and to the patient who depends on it."
