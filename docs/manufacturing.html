What We Can Do

Manufacturing Capabilities

End-to-end pharmaceutical manufacturing capabilities — from sterile and non-sterile formulation through specialist segments to export-grade regulatory compliance.

Sterile Manufacturing

Class A/B cleanroom production of injectables, ophthalmic solutions and sterile suspensions under ISO 5 classified environments.

Non-Sterile Manufacturing

High-volume oral solid dosage manufacturing — tablets, capsules, dry syrups and liquid formulations across multiple production lines.

Beta Lactam

Dedicated, segregated Beta Lactam manufacturing facility with independent air handling, materials flow and cross-contamination controls.

Cephalosporins

Fully segregated cephalosporin manufacturing block with dedicated equipment, personnel gowning and environmental monitoring protocols.

Quality Control

In-house NABL-accredited quality control laboratories at every facility — chemical, microbiological and physical testing from raw material to release.

Packaging

Automated blister packaging, strip packaging, bottle filling and cartonating lines — configured for both domestic and export market specifications.

Export Manufacturing

Export-grade production lines calibrated to destination-market regulatory requirements — USFDA, EU, ANVISA, TGA, WHO Prequalification.

Regulatory Affairs

Integrated regulatory affairs team managing CTD dossier preparation, DMF filings, inspection readiness and ongoing post-approval changes across 40+ markets.

Technology Transfer

Formal technology transfer capabilities — process scale-up, analytical method validation and site-to-site product transfers for CDMO partners.
