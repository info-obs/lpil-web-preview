About Laborate

Four Decades of Pharmaceutical Excellence

Since 1985, Laborate Pharmaceuticals has developed and marketed innovative products that improve, enhance and extend human life — built on a foundation of uncompromising quality and affordability.

With five cutting-edge manufacturing plants and a 5000-strong workforce, we produce millions of tablets, capsules, ampules and vials every day for patients and partners across India and the world.
