A Message from Our Chariman
“Quality is not a department. It is a culture — and that culture begins with the people who lead.”

Laborate was founded on the belief that pharmaceutical manufacturing is not merely an industrial activity — it is a moral responsibility. Every medicine that leaves our facilities carries with it four decades of scientific discipline, quality commitment, and the trust of healthcare providers and patients who depend on us.

Our leadership team brings together expertise across research, manufacturing, regulatory science, and global markets. What unites us is not just professional ambition, but a shared conviction: that every decision we make must ultimately serve the patient. That principle has guided Laborate through four decades of growth, and it will continue to guide us forward.

— Mr. Ajay Bhatia, Chairman, Laborate Pharmaceuticals India Ltd.
How We Think

A Philosophy Built on Scientific Integrity

The principles that guide our leadership decisions were not written in a boardroom. They emerged from 40 years of operating at the intersection of science, manufacturing, and global healthcare.

  1. 01

    Innovation with Discipline

    Scientific progress demands both creative thinking and rigorous validation. We encourage bold ideas, but never at the cost of safety or regulatory integrity.

  2. 02

    Integrity in Every Decision

    Whether at the board level or on the production floor, every decision is made with full transparency. Our stakeholders — patients, partners, and regulators — deserve nothing less.

  3. 03

    Long-Term Thinking

    We have never optimised for the quarter at the expense of the decade. Laborate's growth has been deliberate, sustainable, and rooted in enduring manufacturing quality.

  4. 04

    The Patient as True North

    At every leadership review, the final question is always the same: does this decision serve the patient? That question has never been optional, and it never will be.

Governance History

Building a Framework for Trust

Laborate's governance structures were not adopted as formalities — they were built deliberately, one milestone at a time, to ensure that every product and every decision is held to the highest standards of quality and accountability.

1985

1985

Foundation

Company Founded

Laborate established with a founding board, a single manufacturing plant in Panipat, and a commitment to affordable, quality pharmaceuticals.

1996

1996

Quality Systems

First QMS Established

A formal Quality Management System introduced with dedicated quality assurance personnel, documentation standards, and internal audit protocols.

2003

2003

Compliance

Schedule M Compliance

Full compliance achieved across all product lines with India's revised Schedule M Good Manufacturing Practices, setting operational standards for all future facilities.

2008

2008

Scientific Oversight

Scientific Advisory Board

An independent Scientific Advisory Board constituted, comprising external pharmacologists, regulatory scientists, and analytical chemists to provide objective R&D oversight.

2015

2015

WHO-GMP

All Facilities Certified

WHO-GMP certification achieved across all five manufacturing facilities — a milestone that opened regulated export markets across Africa, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.

2020

2020

Digital QMS

Executive Committee Restructured

The Executive Committee expanded and restructured to include formal sub-committees for Quality, R&D, and Global Markets. Digital quality management system deployed across all sites.

2024

2024

Global Standards

USFDA Audit Clearance

Successful USFDA audit clearance for key facilities. Global governance framework aligned to ICH Q10 pharmaceutical quality system guidelines, strengthening regulatory credibility worldwide.

What We Stand For

Six Principles. One Direction.

These are not aspirational statements. They are the standards by which our leadership team operates and by which we ask every part of our organisation to be measured.

01

Integrity

Every decision grounded in honesty and professional ethics, with no exceptions.

02

Accountability

Every outcome owned by the person responsible for it. Leadership does not delegate blame.

03

Innovation

Scientific curiosity applied to every challenge, balanced by validation and rigour.

04

Patient First

The patient guides every production, quality, and commercial decision we make.

05

Collaboration

Leadership works best when expertise is shared openly across every discipline.

06

Sustainability

Long-term value creation, not short-term optimisation, defines how we plan and act.
