Why Recognition Matters

Not a Trophy Wall. A Body of Evidence.

Every award Laborate has received reflects a judgement made by an independent body — not based on our own claims, but on audit results, export data, quality records, and on-site assessments. That distinction matters.

In the pharmaceutical industry, trust is earned incrementally — through consistent adherence to standards that exist to protect patients. The recognition Laborate has received is not a celebration of achievement. It is a verification that our systems work, that our people are disciplined, and that our commitment to quality is institutional rather than circumstantial.

This page presents that evidence — curated, not catalogued.

Flagship Recognitions

Five Recognitions That Define Our Standard

These are not the only awards Laborate has received. They are the ones that reflect the depth of our commitment — spanning quality systems, export excellence, manufacturing, and workplace culture.

  1. WHO
    PQ

    WHO Prequalification Programme

    World Health Organization

    Laborate products have achieved WHO Prequalification — the most rigorous international quality evaluation for medicines supplied to UN-funded procurement programmes. This designation authorises supply to UNICEF, the Global Fund, and health ministries across 30+ countries. It requires sustained compliance across formulation, manufacturing, stability, and regulatory documentation — verified through on-site WHO audits.

  2. IPA
    2023
    02

    India Pharma Awards — Best Mid-Size Manufacturer

    India Pharma Awards

    Laborate was recognised as India's Best Mid-Size Pharmaceutical Manufacturer — an industry peer evaluation measuring operational excellence, GMP compliance, quality innovation, and export market performance. The recognition reflects a rigorous assessment across production efficiency, regulatory audit outcomes, and sustained growth in regulated international markets.

  3. FICCI
    2022
    03

    FICCI Healthcare Excellence — Quality Award

    Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce & Industry

    FICCI's Healthcare Excellence Award for Quality recognises pharmaceutical organisations that demonstrate a systematic, institutionalised approach to Good Manufacturing Practice — not merely as a compliance requirement, but as a competitive and ethical commitment. Laborate was selected for its sustained GMP record across all five facilities and its consistent export performance in regulated markets.

  4. PEC
    2022
    04

    Pharmexcil Gold Export Award

    Pharmaceuticals Export Promotion Council of India

    The Pharmexcil Gold Award is among the most coveted recognitions for Indian pharmaceutical exporters — awarded for sustained, top-tier growth in regulated international markets. It acknowledges not just export volume, but the quality of the markets served: USFDA-regulated, EU-regulated, and WHO Prequalified. Laborate's consistent presence in these markets is a direct reflection of the integrity of its manufacturing systems.

  5. GPTW
    2023
    05

    Great Place to Work® — India Certified

    Great Place to Work Institute, India

    Pharmaceutical quality is ultimately a human achievement. The Great Place to Work® certification recognises organisations where people experience high levels of trust, pride, and camaraderie — the conditions under which quality-conscious professionals do their best work. Laborate's 2023 certification reflects the alignment between how we treat our people and the standards we hold for our products.

Why It Matters

Recognition as a Signal. Trust as the Outcome.

When a healthcare procurement officer in Nairobi selects a medicine from an approved supplier list, they rely on the same regulatory signals that Laborate has worked to achieve. WHO Prequalification. GMP certification. ISO compliance. These are not brand promises — they are third-party confirmations that a manufacturer operates at the required standard.

For Laborate's customers and partners, recognition means reduced procurement risk. For regulators, it means fewer compliance interventions. For healthcare providers, it means predictable product quality. For patients, it means confidence that the medicine they receive was manufactured with the same rigour that governs any WHO-certified facility worldwide.

That is why Laborate has pursued certification not as a marketing objective, but as an operational standard. Recognition follows when the work is genuinely done.

“We do not pursue awards. We pursue standards. The awards are an independent confirmation that the standards have been met.”

— Laborate Quality Policy
