Why Laborate?

A Workplace Built for Growth

From innovation labs to manufacturing floors, Laborate offers a career environment that challenges, rewards, and invests in you every step of the way.

Innovation Culture

We invest heavily in R&D and encourage every team member to bring new ideas to the table. Innovation is not a department — it’s a mindset.

Career Advancement

Our internal promotion policy ensures that high performers grow. Most of our senior leaders began their careers on the shop floor or in labs.

Global Exposure

Work on products exported to 40+ countries. Participate in WHO, MHRA, and TGA regulatory inspections that give you direct international experience.

Continuous Learning

Structured training programmes, GMP certifications, and leadership development ensure you never stop learning throughout your career at Laborate.

Purposeful Work

Every product we make reaches patients who depend on it. The meaning behind your work here is tangible — you are helping improve and save lives.

Competitive Benefits

Health insurance, PF, gratuity, performance bonuses, and structured leave policies designed to support your wellbeing in and out of work.

Our Values

Principles That Guide Everything We Do

Our six core values are not wall posters — they are the standards we hold each other to every single day.

Quality First

Every product must be safe, effective, and consistent. We hold ourselves to the highest GMP standards in every process, every time.

Patient-Centric

The patient at the end of the supply chain guides every decision we make — from formulation choices to delivery timelines.

Innovation

We invest in science and technology to stay ahead of evolving healthcare needs and continuously expand our product capabilities.

Collaboration

Cross-functional teamwork delivers results no single department can achieve alone. We build bridges across functions, sites, and geographies.

Integrity

We operate with honesty and transparency — in our regulatory submissions, our business dealings, and our conversations with each other.

Sustainability

Responsible manufacturing for society and the environment. We minimise our footprint while maximising the positive impact of every medicine we make.

Gratuity

Performance Rewards
