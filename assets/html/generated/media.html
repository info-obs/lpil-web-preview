Press Release 15 June 2026

Laborate Expands WHO-Prequalified Medicine Portfolio to 35 Export Markets

"Access to quality medicines is not a privilege — it is a responsibility we carry with every shipment we dispatch." — Managing Director, Laborate Pharmaceuticals

Laborate Pharmaceuticals India Ltd. has achieved a significant milestone, expanding its WHO-prequalified medicine portfolio to reach healthcare systems across 35 countries. This expansion follows a series of facility upgrades across our five GMP-certified manufacturing plants in Haryana and Himachal Pradesh, enabling increased production capacity for essential medicines across antimalarial, anti-infective, and cardiovascular therapeutic categories.

The achievement marks a continued commitment to quality-first pharmaceutical manufacturing — ensuring that rigorous global standards are met not as a compliance exercise, but as a reflection of the company's core values.
