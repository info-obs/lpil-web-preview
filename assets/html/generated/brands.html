Brand Ecosystem

A Layered Brand Ecosystem

Laborate manages a portfolio of healthcare brands serving prescription medicines, consumer healthcare, exports and specialised therapeutic portfolios. Each brand has its own market positioning, sales channel and consumer relationship — while sharing Laborate's manufacturing infrastructure, quality systems and regulatory foundation.

Our brand architecture distinguishes between parent brands — independent business entities with their own therapeutic focus — and product brands, which are specific formulations marketed under the Laborate group umbrella. Both tiers benefit from the same WHO-GMP certified manufacturing and four decades of pharmaceutical expertise.

  • Parent brands with independent market positioning
  • 18+ product brands across therapeutic areas
  • Unified manufacturing and quality backbone
  • WHO-GMP and ISO accreditation across the group
